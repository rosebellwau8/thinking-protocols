Provisional hypothesis: the user may learn rule-based systems unusually quickly when feedback is immediate.

- Supporting evidence: three repeated voluntary projects and faster-than-peer onboarding reported in two contexts.
- Counterexample: persistence dropped in a project with delayed feedback.
- Confidence: moderate; feedback is partly self-reported.
- Reality test: try two short tasks with different feedback delays and compare learning speed, energy, and independently scored output.

This is a testable pattern hypothesis, not a personality or clinical conclusion.
